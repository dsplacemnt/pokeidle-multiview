Game

Twitch

Every account keeps live channels of this list open, as many as you choose: the ones where it has the most points first (closest to its next bonus), this list's order deciding between equal ones. Every ~15 minutes each stream briefly loads its full page to grab the bonus chest (+50) and redeem the account's bonus. Empty list = you manage Twitch streams by hand.

    Kick

    Kick pays channel points for 2 lives at a time per account, so every account keeps 2 live channels of this list open and closes its other Kick streams: the ones where it has the most points first, this list's order deciding between equal ones. When one goes offline, the next takes its place (checked every 2 minutes). Empty list = you manage Kick streams by hand.

      Bonus per account

      Redeems the chosen PokeIdle bonus with the account's channel points as soon as they cover it, on its live Kick and Twitch channels (hours add up in the game). The account must have Kick and Twitch linked in the game (Bônus na Kick, Bônus Twitch), or the points are given back.
