Streams

Game

Twitch

Every account keeps live channels of this list open, as many as you choose: the ones where it has the most points first (closest to its next bonus), this list's order deciding between equal ones. Every ~15 minutes each stream briefly loads its full page to grab the bonus chest (+50) and redeem the account's bonus. Empty list = you manage Twitch streams by hand.

    Kick

    Kick pays channel points for 2 lives at a time per account, so every account keeps 2 live channels of this list open and closes its other Kick streams: the ones where it has the most points first, this list's order deciding between equal ones. When one goes offline, the next takes its place (checked every 2 minutes). Empty list = you manage Kick streams by hand.

      Bonus per account

      Spends each account's channel points on PokeIdle bonuses, on its live Kick and Twitch channels. Fixed: always the same bonus. Goals: keeps each bonus above its hours, top first.

      IdleHub vote

      Voting on IdleHub gives the account a diamond every 2 votes (one vote a day counts per nick). Tick the accounts to be reminded: their header shows when the vote is ready, and clicking it opens the vote page in a window with that slot's session, where you sign in and confirm. The app never votes by itself. IdleHub asks for "one person, one account" and may void votes it finds suspicious.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      They work while the app is in front, even with the focus inside a game or stream. Click a shortcut and press the new keys: Ctrl or Alt with a letter or number, or a function key (F1–F12). Esc cancels, Delete turns it off.

      Other keys

      Esc · closes this window Double-click · a slot's name renames it Enter · saves a name or adds a channel; Esc cancels

      Discord alerts

      The app posts to a channel of your Discord server, so you can follow your accounts from the phone. In Discord: channel ⚙ → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL, then paste it here.
